We are NOT creating another Communication Engine.

We already have an Event Bus, Queue, Workflow Engine, Retry Engine, Health Checks, Analytics, and Communication Center.

Your task is to audit, complete, integrate, and validate the existing system so it becomes the single communication backbone for the entire Al Burhan Tours & Travels ERP.
