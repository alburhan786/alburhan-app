Implement an Automatic Payment Reminder System for Al Burhan Tours & Travels.

Requirements:

1. Automatically send payment reminders to customers who have an outstanding balance after booking approval.

2. Reminder schedule:
   - 7 days before due date
   - 3 days before due date
   - 1 day before due date
   - On due date
   - Every 3 days after the due date until the balance is fully paid.

3. Stop sending reminders automatically when the customer's balance becomes ₹0.

4. Send reminders through all enabled channels:
   - SMS (Fast2SMS DLT)
   - WhatsApp (BotBee)
   - Email
   - RCS (if enabled)

5. Use the Fast2SMS DLT Template configured in Admin Settings.
   Route: DLT SMS
   Sender ID: ABURHA

6. Pass these variables:
   Variable 1 = Customer Name
   Variable 2 = Pending Balance Amount

7. SMS / WhatsApp / Email Message:

Assalamu Alaikum {#var#},

Your pending payment of Rs {#var#} for your Hajj/Umrah booking is due.

Please complete your payment to confirm your seat.

For assistance:
+91 9893225590

Warm Regards,
Al Burhan Tours & Travels

8. Create a daily cron job that runs every day at 9:00 AM IST.

9. Before sending reminders, verify:
   - Booking Status = Approved
   - Balance Amount > 0
   - Reminder for the current schedule has not already been sent.

10. Save every reminder in Notification Logs with:
    - Booking ID
    - Customer Name
    - Mobile Number
    - Balance Amount
    - Reminder Type
    - Notification Channel
    - Date & Time
    - Delivery Status
    - Error Message (if any)

11. Add an Admin Panel section called "Payment Reminder Settings" with:
    - Enable/Disable Automatic Reminders
    - Reminder Schedule Settings
    - Send Reminder Now Button
    - Last Reminder Sent
    - Next Reminder Date
    - Total Reminders Sent

12. Add a "Send Payment Reminder" button on every booking page so the admin can manually resend reminders through SMS, WhatsApp, Email, and RCS.

13. Update the Customer Dashboard to display:
    - Pending Balance
    - Due Date
    - Last Reminder Sent
    - Next Reminder Date

14. Prevent duplicate reminders for the same schedule.

15. Build, test, deploy the backend and frontend to the VPS, and verify that automatic payment reminders are working correctly on all notification channels.
