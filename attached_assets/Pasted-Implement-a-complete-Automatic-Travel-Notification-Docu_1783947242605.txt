Implement a complete Automatic Travel Notification & Document Delivery System for Al Burhan Tours & Travels.

OBJECTIVE

Whenever the admin uploads any customer travel document (Flight Ticket, Visa, Hotel Voucher, Tour Itinerary, Model Agreement, Insurance, Room Allotment, Bus Allotment, Haj ID Card, Tax Invoice, Payment Receipt, Passport Copy, Vaccination Certificate, Ziyarat Schedule, etc.), the system must automatically notify the customer via WhatsApp, SMS, Email, and RCS.

----------------------------------------------------
FLIGHT REMINDER AUTOMATION
----------------------------------------------------

Automatically send reminders based on the flight departure date.

Reminder Schedule:

• 7 Days Before Flight
• 3 Days Before Flight
• 2 Days Before Flight
• 1 Day Before Flight
• 12 Hours Before Flight
• 6 Hours Before Flight
• 3 Hours Before Flight

Stop reminders automatically after the flight departs.
