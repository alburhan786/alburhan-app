PHASE 16 – ENTERPRISE REQUIREMENTS

The ERP must be event-driven.

Every action should automatically:

* Update the database
* Update dashboards
* Generate PDFs
* Send notifications through all configured channels
* Record audit logs
* Track delivery status
* Retry failures automatically
* Keep customer and admin portals synchronized in real time

No manual intervention should be required after the initial configuration.
