We need a permanent production-grade notification system for Al Burhan Tours & Travels.

Current situation:

• Booking notifications are inconsistent.
• Payment notifications sometimes fail.
• WhatsApp, Email and SMS are not always triggered.
• OTP occasionally fails.
• Invoice status and Agreement synchronization have had issues.
• SMS currently works only because Quick Route fallback was restored.

This is NOT the desired production architecture.

=========================
PRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS
=========================

Implement one centralized Notification Service.

Every event must pass through one notification pipeline.

Notification Service
        ↓
SMS
WhatsApp

Audit Log
