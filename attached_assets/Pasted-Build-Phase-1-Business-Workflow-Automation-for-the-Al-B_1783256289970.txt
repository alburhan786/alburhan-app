Build Phase 1 - Business Workflow Automation for the Al Burhan Tours & Travels ERP.

IMPORTANT:

- Do NOT break any existing functionality.
- Do NOT delete any data.
- Use the existing Notification Engine.
- Every workflow must execute automatically.
- Log every workflow execution.
- Allow Admin to enable or disable each automation.
