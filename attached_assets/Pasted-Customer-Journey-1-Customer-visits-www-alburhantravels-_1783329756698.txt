Customer Journey

1. Customer visits www.alburhantravels.com.
2. Customer registers or logs in using Mobile OTP, Email OTP, or WhatsApp OTP.
3. Customer receives a welcome message by:
    * WhatsApp
    * SMS
    * Email
4. Customer browses Hajj, Umrah, Iraq, Syria, Jordan, and Ziyarat packages.
5. Customer books a package.
6. Admin receives an instant notification.
7. Customer receives:
    * Booking ID
    * Booking Confirmation
    * Booking Status
    via WhatsApp, SMS, Email, and dashboard.

Final Objective

The Al Burhan Tours & Travels website should provide a complete digital pilgrimage experience where every stage—from registration and booking to payment, document verification, visa processing, ticket issuance, travel updates, and journey completion—is fully automated. Customers should receive real-time updates through WhatsApp, SMS, email, push notifications, and the dashboard, while administrators manage all operations from a single secure control panel with complete CRM, reporting, and automation. This should be a production-ready, enterprise-grade platform suitable for thousands of pilgrims.
