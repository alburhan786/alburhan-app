# MASTER TASK – BUILD ENTERPRISE CENTRAL COMMUNICATION & AUTOMATION ENGINE

You are the Lead Enterprise Software Architect for the Al Burhan Tours & Travels ERP/CRM.

Your mission is to design and implement a production-ready, enterprise-grade Central Communication & Automation Engine that becomes the single source of truth for all communications throughout the ERP.

The architecture must be inspired by Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Shopify, and Oracle NetSuite.

This is NOT a simple notification module.

This must become the communication backbone of the entire ERP.

====================================================
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
====================================================

No module in the ERP may send WhatsApp, SMS, Email, Push Notifications, Dashboard Notifications, or Webhooks directly.

Every ERP module must only publish an event.

The Central Communication & Automation Engine will process every event and execute all workflows automatically.

Remove duplicated notification logic across the application.
